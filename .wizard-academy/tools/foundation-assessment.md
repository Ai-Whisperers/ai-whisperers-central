## 💡 ASSESSMENT TIPS

### **For Accurate Assessment:**
1. **Be honest** - This is for your growth, not judgment
2. **Think of recent examples** - Not ideal scenarios
3. **Consider patterns** - Not one-time exceptions
4. **Get external perspective** - Ask others how they see you

### **Common Assessment Errors:**
- **Overestimating** - We often think we're more exponential than we are
- **Context confusion** - You might be exponential in one area, linear in another
- **Ideal vs actual** - Assessing what you aspire to vs what you actually do
- **Recent bias** - Overweighting recent experiences

### **Improvement Tracking:**
- Take assessment every 4 weeks
- Track section scores over time
- Note specific question improvements
- Connect improvements to practices
