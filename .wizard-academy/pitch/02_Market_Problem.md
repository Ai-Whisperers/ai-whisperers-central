# Market & Problem

The Netherlands hosts thousands of SMEs drowning in manual workflows, outdated software, and disconnected systems. Service industries—logistics, professional services, and trade—are most burdened: the average team spends over 100 hours/month on routine tasks that sap creativity and slow growth.

Legacy processes are "lead"—heavy, slow, and silent anchors. The market is fragmented with patchwork SaaS magicians, none offering a transformative story or doctrine. Ay-Eye Whisperers identifies the mine beneath the graveyard—companies ready for transmutation.
