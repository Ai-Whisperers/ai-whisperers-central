## 🏺 Strategic Summary (The Alchemical Origin)

### 🎣 The Fishing Metaphor
The company's origin is visualized through a person fishing who identifies a massive, powerful entity (The Wizard Tower) hidden beneath the surface. This represents identifying true talent and hidden potential in a "bloody feeding frenzy" of a market.

### 📜 The Guild & The Masterpiece
The "Wizard Academy" operates as a Guild. 
*   **Doctrine:** Students must learn from outside the "house" to maintain standards.
*   **The Masterpiece:** Mastery is proven through the creation of a definitive work (like this project).
*   **The Goal:** Not just selling projects, but selling the "Magic" (AI transformation).

### 💎 Turning Cold to Diamonds
The mission is to take legacy companies ("Sleeping Turtles") and inject an AI brain to superpower them, creating a "Factory of Magic Staffs."
